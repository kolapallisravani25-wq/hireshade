6. Do we offer artificial intelligence-based products?

In Short: We offer products, features, or tools powered by artificial intelligence, machine learning, or similar technologies.

As part of our Services, we offer products, features, or tools powered by artificial intelligence, machine learning, or similar technologies (collectively, “AI Products”). These tools are designed to enhance your experience and provide you with innovative solutions. The terms in this Privacy Notice govern your use of the AI Products within our Services.

Use of AI Technologies

We provide the AI Products through third-party service providers (“AI Service Providers”): OpenAI, Google, Anthropic, Amazon Web Services, Cerebras, Speechmatics and Pinecone. Your input, the output generated from it, and any personal information contained in either are shared with and processed by these AI Service Providers to enable your use of our AI Products, for the purposes outlined in “WHAT LEGAL BASES DO WE RELY ON TO PROCESS YOUR PERSONAL INFORMATION?” You must not use the AI Products in any way that violates the terms or policies of any AI Service Provider.

What each AI Product sends, and to whom

  • Call Assistant. Your microphone or call audio is streamed to Speechmatics, which returns a transcript. So that names and specialist terms are spelled correctly, Speechmatics also receives a short list of them taken from the session: the company, and words from your resume, job description and notes, chosen by a model served by Amazon Web Services, or by Google if that model is unavailable. If you set up a voice profile, your voice sample is processed by Speechmatics to derive the voice signature, and that signature is sent to Speechmatics at the start of each call it applies to. The transcript, together with the resume, job description, notes and any screenshots you attach to a session, is sent to the AI model you select for that session, which is served by OpenAI, Google, Anthropic (directly or through Amazon Web Services) or Cerebras. Session titles and summaries are generated by a model served by Amazon Web Services, or by Google if that model is unavailable.
  • Documents. Documents you attach to a session are split into passages that are embedded, stored and searched through Pinecone, in a partition reserved for your account, so that relevant passages can be included in the content sent to the model during a call.
  • Mock Interview. Your microphone audio and the interview conversation are processed by OpenAI, which also voices the interviewer, and are transcribed by Speechmatics as in a real call.
  • Resume Maker. The resume you build, upload or paste, the job details you target it at, and the messages you type in its chat are sent to models served by Google, OpenAI and Amazon Web Services. A profile photo stored on a resume is removed before that resume is sent to a model; where you import a resume by uploading its file, the file is sent to the model as it is, so any photograph printed on its pages is sent with it. A LinkedIn profile URL you import is sent to Enrich Layer, and a job posting URL to Firecrawl, to fetch the page for you; neither is an AI provider.
  • Headshots. The photo you upload, and the look you choose, are sent to Google to generate your headshots.
  • Question Bank. Questions are extracted from the interviewer side of ended, English, paid interview sessions whose transcript you chose to save, by a model served by OpenAI, or by Google if that model is unavailable. The extracted questions are then sorted into categories by a model served by OpenAI, or by Google if that model is unavailable, and those in a category the bank lists are grouped and titled by models served by Google, or by OpenAI if those models are unavailable. The job title the interview was for is read from the same transcript and rewritten into a standard role name by a model served by Amazon Web Services, or by Google if that model is unavailable, so that interviews for the same role can be grouped. You can stop this, and remove what was already contributed, from your profile under Privacy, or at any time by asking us at support@parakeet-ai.com to opt you out.
  • Reviewing us for free credits. Where you publish a review of us on another site and upload a screenshot of it to claim the credit, that screenshot is sent to a model served by Google, or by OpenAI if that model is unavailable, to check that the review is published and that it is yours. What the model reads back — the reviewer name shown, the rating and whether the review is positive — is stored with the record of that claim.
  • Support chat. Your messages to the support assistant, and any screenshots you attach to them, are sent to a model from Anthropic, or from Google if that model is unavailable; our team sees the screenshots too when it reads the chat. While you are signed in, they go with facts from your account so the assistant can help: your plan, credits, purchases and refunds, whether paid features or payments are blocked, your recent calls (their name, device, versions, meeting app, settings and how each went), your Resume Maker resumes, headshots and Question Bank companies, and your reports and requests to us; when you ask about a call's answers, that call's questions and answers go too. Each conversation is also sorted by topic by a model from Amazon Web Services, or from Google if that model is unavailable. A request the assistant passes to our team is read by a person, who may use an AI model from Anthropic to help answer it.
  • Feedback. What you write in the Feedback form or in a comment about a call or one of its answers, a problem you report in the support chat, and one you report by email, is sent to Google to find the existing tickets it may match, and to a model served by Google, or by OpenAI if that model is unavailable, which files it with one of those tickets or opens a new one. When we know whose report it is, that model also reads facts from your account when it files your report: your plan, credit balance, whether paid features are blocked, purchases, refunds and credit returns from the last 60 days, what each review of us would pay you, and, for your latest calls and the call your feedback is about, the device, app and system version and meeting app used, the settings, and how each call went, never what was said in them. Our team checks where each report was filed and may use an AI model from Anthropic to help, which reads the report, the ticket it is on and those facts. When you mark answers from a call, our team can see those answers.

Your email address and payment details (card or payment method, and amounts) are not sent to an AI Service Provider on their own; a provider receives personal details only where they appear in the content you give an AI Product, such as the contact block of a resume or what is said during a call, and in the account facts that file and check your feedback (above).

If you decline, you are signed out and nothing is sent. On the web and desktop apps, you provide content to an AI Product by using it, and this section describes what happens to that content. You can withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us at support@parakeet-ai.com or by deleting your account, as described under “HOW CAN YOU REVIEW, UPDATE, OR DELETE THE DATA WE COLLECT FROM YOU?”
